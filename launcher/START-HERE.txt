COURSE NOTEBOOKS - HOW TO START THEM
====================================

You do not install anything and you never type anything. Everything is done by double-clicking.


STEP 1 - UNZIP
--------------
Unzip the file you downloaded. Put the folder anywhere you like.
  Windows: right-click the zip file, choose "Extract All", then "Extract".
  Mac:     double-click the zip file.
Do not start anything from inside the zip file. It only holds a few files, so this takes seconds.


STEP 2 - START
--------------
WINDOWS
  Double-click "Start" (it is called Start.bat).
  If Windows says "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway".

MAC
  Double-click "Start.command".
  The first time, your Mac says it cannot check this file for malicious software. This is normal.
    1. Click "Done" (do NOT click "Move to Trash").
    2. Open the Apple menu, then "System Settings", then "Privacy & Security".
    3. Scroll down. You see a message about "Start.command". Click "Open Anyway", then "Open".
       Your Mac may ask for your password.
    (On macOS 14 or older you can instead right-click "Start.command", choose "Open", then "Open" again.)
  You only do this once.


STEP 3 - WAIT, THE FIRST TIME ONLY
----------------------------------
A window opens. The very first time it says "Setting up the course tools".
This takes a few minutes. Just wait and keep the window open.
Then a page asks whether your analyses may be shared with the course (see "Sharing your analyses" below),
and then your browser shows the list of notebooks. Click the one you need.
The next times, it starts in a few seconds.


STEP 4 - WORK
-------------
Keep the window open while you work. Closing the window stops the notebooks.
Closing the browser tab does NOT stop them: double-click Start again and the tab comes back.


STOPPING, AND STARTING AGAIN
----------------------------
To stop:   close the window that opened in step 2 (the browser tab can stay or go).
To start:  double-click Start again. It is always safe to double-click it again, at any time:
             - if the notebooks are not running, it starts them;
             - if they are already running, it just opens the page again;
             - if the first setup was cut short, it finishes it (see below).
Your work is saved under your key, so you lose nothing when you stop.


IF YOU CLOSE THE LAPTOP, IT GOES TO SLEEP, OR IT SHUTS DOWN
-----------------------------------------------------------
- Sleep, or lid closed: the notebooks just pause. When you wake the laptop, reload the page in your browser.
  If the page still says it is disconnected: close the window, then double-click Start again.
- Shut down, restart, battery empty, or a crash: the notebooks are gone, nothing keeps running in the background.
  Double-click Start again.
- This also holds in the middle of the first setup. Nothing is broken: double-click Start again and it
  finishes the setup (it may start the setup over, so allow a few minutes again). Please do not open Start twice
  at the same time while the very first setup is running.


YOUR KEY
--------
"01 Initial Analysis" and "02 Map Explorer" ask for the key your teachers gave you. Paste it once. Your work is saved
under it and comes back whenever you use the same key, on any computer.


SHARING YOUR ANALYSES (OPTIONAL)
--------------------------------
The first time you start, a page asks whether your analyses may be shared with the course, anonymised:
only the analysis steps you run, not your clicks. They are used to improve the course and for research on tools
that support exploratory analysis. You answer once and are not asked again. If you say no, everything works the
same; your work just stays on your computer.

To change your answer: double-click "Change-my-answer" (Change-my-answer.bat on Windows, Change-my-answer.command
on a Mac, next to Start). On a Mac, the first time, do the same "Open Anyway" step as for Start.command.
If the notebooks are running, close their window first. The page then asks again.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
- A red message ("Failed to fetch", "Unauthorized", lost connection), or a page that does not respond:
  reload the page. Your work comes back as you left it. If the key is asked again, paste it again.
- "Access is denied", or it will not start, on a laptop managed by your university: the laptop's security
  policy can block a new program. Ask your IT department to allow this folder:
    Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\hs-marimo26
    Mac:     ~/Library/Application Support/hs-marimo26
  Nothing in it needs administrator rights.
- Still stuck? Take a photo of the window and send it to your teachers.


UPDATES
-------
Each time you start, the notebooks fetch the latest version by themselves when you are online.
You only need to download the zip again if the window tells you so.


THE EVENT LOGS
--------------
They are in the "notebooks/data" folder inside the folder the course tools were set up in:
  Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\hs-marimo26\runtime\notebooks\data
  Mac:     ~/Library/Application Support/hs-marimo26/runtime/notebooks/data
- Dhana loan application: dhana-loan-application.xes, dhana-loan-application-molab.xes, dhana-loan-application.csv
  (the notebooks themselves read dhana-loan-application.parquet, the same log in a format that loads in seconds).
- Purchasing-Example.csv, SepsisCases2020EventLog.xes.
